# ADR-0025: Budget, IPS and decision-engine conventions (Phase 5)

Status: Accepted, 2026-11-12. Fills gaps in B1-B6 and F3; changes no Part A invariant. Reversible by the investor.

## Decisions

**Shape.** `policy` is pure (no HTTP, no `arthashreni.db`, no web framework; import-linter), except `policy/store.py`, the only module that touches the database. `src/arthashreni/review.py` (like `portfolio.py`) is the service layer that loads a dataset and calls `analytics`, `risk` and `policy`. `risk` never imports `policy`. The LLM is not involved: nothing in `policy`, `risk` or `reports` may import an HTTP client.

**The envelope** (ADR-0009 holds; two refinements). `budget_periods` has the four columns and the CHECK. `envelope_lines` carry kinds `SIP`, `NEW_ALLOCATION`, `RESERVE_TRANSFER` and `RESERVE_USE`. There is **no `UNALLOCATED` line**: the remainder is by definition not allocated, so the identity `committed + new + reserve transfers + unallocated = opening` is checked on the columns, and the deferred trigger checks that line sums for the first three kinds equal their columns. `RESERVE_USE` spends money set aside in earlier months (B2), so it sits **outside** the month's identity; it is explicit, separate and approved like any line. A month goes `OPEN -> ALLOCATED` once; an allocated month, its lines and its recommendation are frozen (database triggers). There is one recommendation per month. `review run` without `--commit` stores nothing, so the investor can re-run freely; `--commit` is the single, explicit step that stores the month.

**Concurrency.** `allocate_period` takes `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on the month's row first; the second of two simultaneous allocations waits, then sees `ALLOCATED` and is refused (`PeriodAllocated`). The triggers are the backstop. Tested with two threads over four months.

**Running SIPs** are pre-committed: a mandate is a row (history by `supersedes_id`), active in a month when `ACTIVE` and its dates overlap the month. Their amounts leave the budget before free money is considered, and they count as money already moving into their assets when bands are checked. SIP total above the budget is a configuration error (`OverAllocated`), never silently trimmed. A SIP into an asset with a veto stays in the envelope (it is a mandate at the broker, which this system never cancels) and gets a `review` line.

**Leftover cash** (B2, ADR-0017). Unallocated money is swept into the cash reserve only at `review close`, once per month (`sweep_period` unique index). The next month still opens at the budget in force (`budget_settings`, newest effective row), so leftovers never inflate it. Reserve entries are append-only and a trigger refuses any entry that would make a balance, or blocked IPO money, negative (advisory lock per reserve). Spending a reserve needs an approved `USE` line. Sale proceeds and dividends are entered into the reserve (`reserve entry`), not the budget.

**IPS** is a Pydantic object stored as JSONB in `ips_versions`: DRAFT, APPROVED, SUPERSEDED. A draft is never used. Approval needs a note, supersedes the previous approved version and happens only through the CLI (the investor). Approved bodies cannot be edited (trigger); a change is a new version. Bands are fractions of the portfolio and must be able to sum to 100%. `theme` limits exist in the schema but are not enforceable: assets have no theme attribute (known limit). Defaults are conservative (cost threshold 1%, sales only beyond a 5-point margin, three-month cooldown). Advisory weights stay 25/25/20/15/15, labelled hypotheses.

**Decision engine order** (F3): hard gates, policy allocation, vetoes, advisory score, cost rule, envelope validator. Gates: approved IPS; clean reconciliation; liquidity (emergency fund known and at least the IPS months; planned expense window; income stability); usable price per asset (`stale`, `missing`, `conflicting` and `quarantined` mean "insufficient evidence" for that asset, not an approximation). Unknown emergency fund blocks new money (insufficient evidence); a shortfall sends money to the cash reserve first. **Policy allocation** works in tiers (below band low, then below band mid) and the score only orders inside a tier, so a score can never outrank a band. Money to a class is capped by: its band target (mid), band high, country and currency band highs, holding, fund-house and sector limits, the monthly mix-change limit (half the sum of absolute class-weight changes since the start of the month, found by bisection), and, when income is not confirmed stable, "the risky share of the portfolio may not rise". After each allocation weights are recomputed. Classes counted as risk money: equity, equity ETFs, mutual funds and silver (V32). Reserve use is held to the same band, concentration, data and cost limits (except the class target, since the investor chose the asset); a property test found it had skipped them in a first version, and that is fixed.

**Sales are never implied.** A class above its band high by more than the margin gets a `review` line, or `reduce` only when the investor flagged the thesis as failed; either way the line says a sale needs tax, cost, thesis and alternatives, and that tax is unavailable (no verified rule, C7). Cooldown: an asset class reduced within the last N months cannot be accumulated.

**Cost rule** (B4). Schedules are the investor's input in TOML (`config/costs.toml` for REAL; `demo/costs.toml` is invented round numbers for DEMO). **None is hardcoded.** A missing schedule makes cost unknown, which means "do not invest this month": the money goes to the cash reserve with the reason. Cost above the IPS threshold also goes to the reserve with the amount at which it becomes acceptable. Below the minimum ticket, the candidate is skipped and no money is diverted. Stamp duty and NAV applicability (V16) stay open: the schedule carries `stamp_duty_pct` as the investor enters it.

**IPO sinking fund** (B3). `ipo_applications` records board and the minimum lot value (from the RHP, never hardcoded); SME is stored as `EXCLUDED` (also a CHECK), and `ipo_verdict` returns false for anything that is not MAINBOARD. "Can apply" needs the reserve to cover the minimum and the research classification to be `ALLOWED` (default `UNRATED`, until Phase 7). ASBA block, allotment and refund are separate reserve events (`IPO_BLOCK`, `IPO_ALLOT`, `IPO_RELEASE`); blocked money counts as committed until released. The matching ledger transactions (`IPO_*` types) arrive with statement imports, as before.

**Decisions** (`decisions`, append-only, reason required). Recording a decision executes nothing: the only side effect is the investor's own reserve bookkeeping (an approved reserve transfer or use writes a reserve entry once). `human_approval_required` is a CHECK-constrained constant true.

**Confidence** (ADR-0010) was specified but not built in Phase 3; `dq/confidence.py` implements it now. Cross-checking means a second source priced the asset on its latest date. With none, agreement is 0 and confidence is `low`: honest for DEMO and for early real months.

**Reconciliation** blocks the month: any unexplained difference on the latest statement makes the engine allocate nothing.

## Consequences
Over-allocation is impossible, not warned. With no approved IPS the output is only running SIPs plus a list of what is missing. DEMO output is labelled DEMO in the recommendation itself (validated), and the DEMO policy lives only in the `demo` schema.
