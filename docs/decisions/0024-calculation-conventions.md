# ADR-0024: Calculation conventions (Phase 4)

Status: Accepted, 2026-11-11. Fills gaps in F1 and G1.5; changes no Part A invariant.

## Decisions

**Decimal everywhere.** `analytics` has no `float` (enforced by `scripts/check_no_float.py`). Every multiply, divide, power and root runs in a 40-digit context set by the function itself (`PRECISION`, defined in `dq/measured.py`, which also wraps every `m_add`/`m_mul`/`m_div` helper), never in the caller's ambient context; `test_results_do_not_depend_on_the_callers_decimal_context` runs twr, absolute return, CAGR, positions and valuation under precisions 5 to 80 and requires identical results. Sums of ledger amounts are exact up to 28 digits in any context; the documented "XIRR boundary" exception in `docs/verify-design.md` is not needed and is unused. Exact functions (sums, products, FX attribution) are exact to that precision; the oracle tolerance of 1e-6 is a test tolerance, not a property of the code.

**Shape.** `analytics` is pure (no database, no HTTP; import-linter contract). Inputs are `Measured` (prices, FX) or plain ledger values; outputs are `Measured`. `src/arthashreni/portfolio.py` is the only place that loads the ledger and market data and calls `analytics` (`build_report`); the API router and the CLI call it. `mypy --strict` makes a bare number a type error wherever a price or rate is expected.

**XIRR** (`analytics/xirr.py`). Actual/365 from the first flow date (Excel's convention). Flows on the same day are netted. Solved in `x = ln(1 + r)` by scanning for a sign change and bisecting, in Decimal. It never guesses; it returns `missing` with the reason when:
- fewer than two dates, or flows do not change sign;
- no root exists between -99.9% and +1,200,000% a year;
- more than one rate solves the flows (including a tangent root, see below), or the only root has even multiplicity. Other tools return one root arbitrarily; we refuse.

*What is proved and what is only checked* (corrected after review; the first version of this ADR overstated Norstrom):
- **Descartes' rule of signs** (valid for real exponents): if the *flows* change sign exactly once there is exactly one root, simple, for every rate above -100%. This is the only case returned `ok`. It covers the usual investor pattern: money in, then money out and a final value.
- **Norstrom's criterion**: if the *running total* changes sign exactly once and the total is not zero, there is exactly one root for rates **above 0%**. It says nothing about negative rates. Counterexample (in `tests/test_xirr.py`): flows -8, -1, +28, -13 at years 0, 3, 4, 5 have a running total -8, -9, +19, +6 (one sign change) and NPV zero at about -52.8% and +16.2%. So Norstrom narrows the search but never earns `ok`; a Norstrom-only pattern is `estimated` with the note "unique above 0% by Norstrom; negative rates checked by scan only". Tested as a property (1,000 random patterns: at most one positive root when it applies).
- **Everything else** is checked by a 5% log-rate scan that counts crossings, plus a refinement at every local extremum of the NPV between grid points: the true extremum is located by bisecting the derivative, and if it has the opposite sign two crossings were hiding in one step, and if it is zero to within 1e-18 of the present values the NPV touches zero without crossing (**even multiplicity**). A tangent root next to a crossing root counts as two roots, so a sign-change-only scan can no longer return the crossing root as if it were the answer. Exactly one crossing and nothing hidden is returned `estimated` ("uniqueness not proved; checked by scan only"). Limit: two stationary points inside one 0.05 step would not be resolved.
- Tested: `test_a_returned_root_is_never_one_of_several` (1,000 random sign patterns against an independent 0.01-grid root counter: two or more crossings always give `missing`; `ok` only when the flows change sign once; a returned value matches the independent root).

**Which cash flows count** (`analytics/flows.py`). Investor view: BUY, SIP, SELL, REDEMPTION, DIVIDEND, INTEREST, FEE, TAX, IPO_BLOCK, IPO_REFUND (an IPO allotment has no cash of its own: block plus refund equal its cost, ADR-0022 §8). Not counted: DEPOSIT, WITHDRAWAL, FX_CONVERSION (bank-to-broker plumbing, not investment results) and unit moves without cash (switch, transfer, reinvested dividend), which are neutral for the portfolio as a whole. An *asset-level* XIRR for an asset with such moves is `estimated` and says so (a transfer in from another broker carries no cost here). A merger folds the predecessor's flows into the successor so one holding has one return.

**Returns, each naming itself and why** (`ReturnMeasure`):
- *XIRR*: money-weighted; the investor chooses when to add money, so timing counts. Default headline.
- *TWR*: chain-linked sub-period returns, flows at the end of their day: `(V_t - flow_t) / V_(t-1)`. **External-flow semantics** (`flows.twr_flow`): the valued portfolio is the holdings only (no cash balance), so the flow on a day is minus the investor's cash flows that day. A purchase or IPO block is money added (positive); a sale, redemption or IPO refund is money taken out (negative); a dividend or interest payment leaves the holdings as cash (negative), which is why income shows up as return; a standalone fee or tax is paid in from outside and buys no value (positive), so it lowers the return. Deposits, withdrawals and FX conversions (bank to broker cash) are not flows, and neither are unit moves without cash (switch, transfer, reinvested dividend). Tested in `test_twr_counts_dividends_as_return_and_fees_as_cost`. A sub-period that starts at zero value is skipped. Cumulative, not annualised. **Partial valuations are refused:** if the valuation on any date left out a holding for want of a price (`Totals.unvalued`, passed as `ValuePoint.complete=False`), TWR is `missing` and names the dates. Chaining through it would read the absent holding as a loss on that date and a gain on the next date it is priced, and the error compounds, so an `estimated` label would hide a wrong number. XIRR with a partial ending value stays `estimated` with the note, because a single end value is a bounded understatement, not a compounding one (`tests/test_portfolio_partial_twr.py`). It needs a price on every flow date: without one it is `missing` (it never substitutes another instrument or zero). The DEMO market data starts in 2026, the demo ledger in 2024, so DEMO TWR is `missing`; `tests/test_portfolio_twr.py` proves it on a ledger whose flow dates all have prices.
- *Absolute return*: total gain over money paid in. Gain = ending value + sum of counted flows (so it includes dividends, fees and taxes recorded as rows) and is **before any tax**: tax output stays "tax unavailable" until verified rules exist (C7).
- *CAGR*: one lump sum between two dates only.

**FX attribution** (`analytics/fx.py`). `(1 + r_inr) = (1 + r_local)(1 + r_fx)`, so `r_inr = r_local + r_fx + r_local * r_fx`, and in INR `local = (V - C) f0`, `fx = C (f1 - f0)`, `interaction = (V - C)(f1 - f0)`, summing to `V f1 - C f0` exactly. `f0` is the *blended* rate the ledger recorded (the broker's rate for INDmoney trades), `f1` the valuation rate with its type named (ADR-0016: FBIL reference, else SBI TT buying marked `estimated`). The broker's conversion markup therefore shows up inside the FX effect. `residual` is reported and tested against 1e-12.

**Partial valuations.** A holding with no usable price or FX (missing or quarantined) is excluded from totals and weights, listed by name, and the totals/weights become at least `estimated` with a note. Totals are never silently shorter and never silently `missing` because of one holding.

**Series statistics** (`analytics/series.py`): simple returns between consecutive observations; volatility is the sample standard deviation x sqrt(252), which assumes **one return per trading day**. That is checked, not assumed: a series whose median gap (the true median: the mean of the middle two for an even count) exceeds 5 calendar days (weekend plus a holiday) is refused (`missing`, "not a daily series"), and a daily series with a longer gap is `estimated` with the number of gaps, because a return across a gap spans several sessions. Correlation and benchmark comparison first reduce both series to the dates they share, so each pair of returns covers the same interval in both series; a day that only one series has cannot affect the result (tested, including against pairing by position); drawdown is the worst peak-to-trough fall with peak, trough and recovery dates (and distance from the running peak today); correlation and benchmark comparison use the dates both series share. At least 20 returns are required, otherwise `missing` with the count. Unusable points are dropped and the result is `estimated` with a count; otherwise the status is the worst of the points used. `as_of` is the last observation date. The report uses the last 365 days per holding.

**Benchmarks** (`analytics/benchmarks.py`) are a documented list with a rationale each (Nifty 50 TRI, S&P 500 TR in USD, gold and silver in INR). No index level is fetched (no compliant source in the registry, D1). The investor registers the index as an asset (class `ETF_IN`, the ticker named in the list, never held) and imports its levels with `market import-prices`; without them the comparison is `missing`. DEMO ships a synthetic Nifty-like series; there is no S&P series, so the US holding's comparison is `missing`.

**Look-through and overlap** are pure functions over a mapping of fund holdings. No fund-holdings data exists until Phase 7, so every fund is listed as "no holdings data", the exposure is `estimated`, and overlap is `missing`.

## Not built (stated, not hidden)
- Portfolio-level volatility and drawdown from reconstructed daily portfolio values (holding-level is built). Needs a daily price and FX panel; planned with the risk engine (Phase 5).
- Tax on realised gains (no verified rule, C7); inflation-adjusted returns; per-account returns.

## Verification
- `scripts/xirr_oracle.py`: 12 cash-flow cases, ours vs `pyxirr` vs LibreOffice `XIRR` (headless) within 1e-6, or all three report no solution. Multiple-root cases are excluded on purpose.
- Golden: `tests/golden/derive_calcs.py` re-derives the DEMO valuation, P&L, income and costs, XIRR (portfolio and per asset, by float bisection on the rate: a different method), FX attribution, allocation and concentration from the committed demo files without importing `arthashreni`; `make verify` fails if `expected_calcs.json` is stale; `tests/test_phase4_gate.py` compares the engine to it.
- Property tests (`hypothesis`, 1,000 examples in `make verify`): weights sum to 1; value = units x price x FX; FX parts add up; TWR ignores contribution timing; drawdown bounds; correlation bounds; XIRR root has zero NPV.
- `mutmut` on `analytics` and the pure `ledger` modules; threshold in `docs/verify-design.md`.

## Consequences
Every figure in the CLI and API shows value, status, as-of and notes. A calculation that cannot be done reliably says so instead of returning a number.
